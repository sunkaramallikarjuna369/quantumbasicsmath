The Fundamental Single-Qubit Quantum Gates

What are Pauli Gates?

The Pauli gates (X, Y, Z) are the most fundamental single-qubit gates in quantum computing. They correspond to rotations of π (180°) around the X, Y, and Z axes of the Bloch sphere. Together with the identity I, they form a complete basis for all 2×2 Hermitian matrices.

Gate Properties

Self-inverse: X² = Y² = Z² = I

Anti-commutation: XY = -YX, YZ = -ZY, ZX = -XZ

Products: XY = iZ, YZ = iX, ZX = iY

Hermitian: X† = X, Y† = Y, Z† = Z

Unitary: X†X = Y†Y = Z†Z = I
